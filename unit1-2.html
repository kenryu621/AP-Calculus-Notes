Discontinuities

Hole

A hole is a open circle on the graph, it could occur when a situation like a piece-wise function have f(X) ≠ y.

Non-removable Hole

A non-removable hole is just like a normal hole, but the f(X) is equal to another y-value that is appointed.

Vertical Asymptote

A v.a will occur when there is a denominator. When the denominator equal 0, no y-value could be found (±infinites for denominator that has even power.)

Jump

A jump is piecewise function, if x is where a jump occur, f(x) could be defined but the limit of f(x) will be DNE, only one sided limit can be valid.

IVT

The Intermediate Value Theorem is a process of verifying is f(x) in the interval between f(a) and f(b) or not.

The interval has to be continuous.
